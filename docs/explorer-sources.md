# Sri Lanka Explorer sources

- Outline: Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 countries, public domain. Sri Lanka feature extracted from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson . Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . The map and markers share a local equirectangular projection with cosine correction at 7.8° N. Marker positions use longitude / latitude, while leader lines connect offset labels for readability. The decorative contour ellipses are illustrative, not terrain data.
- General heritage: Sri Lanka Tourism, https://www.srilanka.travel/cultural_heritage and https://www.srilanka.travel/heritage-discover-the-past .
- Ella landmarks: Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority Ella planning documents, https://www.sltda.gov.lk/storage/common_media/ELLA_VOLUME_1_Part_B_compressed2395158019.pdf .
- Udawalawe: Sabaragamuwa Provincial Tourism, https://tourism.sg.gov.lk/udawalawa/ .
- Galle photo: Grayswoodsurrey, “Galle Fort, Sri Lanka.JPG”, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Galle_Fort,_Sri_Lanka.JPG , CC BY-SA 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Resized and converted to WebP; CSS crops to fit. Attribution is displayed on the Galle card and detail view. The adapted photo remains CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Other photos: existing user-supplied website assets. Yala and Udawalawe photos illustrate wildlife rather than guaranteeing sightings. Existing south-coast photograph for Unawatuna is regionally illustrative.
